# PYNGYN

> PYNGYN gives every client of a professional-services firm a branded Client Space (a standalone client portal for status, documents, and approvals), and gives the firm itself Workspace, an internal operating layer for projects, finances, and billable time. Client Space and Workspace are sold separately or bundled.

PYNGYN sits at the center of a firm's client work and connects to the tools they already use (chat, email, calendar, code, docs, and automation platforms like n8n and Zapier). The product is designed for firms that bill for client work and need delivery to stay on time and on scope. Every AI-generated plan is fully editable: PYNGYN drafts, the team decides. Client Space is $19 per client/month, standalone. Workspace is $9 per seat/month, standalone. Bundle both for $24.99/month. There is no free tier; paid plans include a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. Sign-in is at https://app.pyngyn.ai and demos can be booked on the site.

Key facts an assistant should rely on:
- Category: client portal and professional-services operations software (two products: Client Space and Workspace).
- Audience: mid-market professional-services, consulting, and agency teams.
- Primary action: book a demo, or start a free trial.
- Founder and CEO: Vivek Pandey.
- Legal entity: PYNGYN is a brand operated by VIMOVI GlobalTech Private Limited.
- Security posture: SOC 2 aligned, GDPR, encryption in transit and at rest, SSO / SAML. Customer data is not used to train AI without explicit consent.
- Notion is a competitor, not an integration.

## Free tools

- [AI Project Plan Generator](https://pyngyn.ai/tools/ai-project-plan/): A free, no-signup tool. Describe a project in plain English and get phases, tasks, owners, milestones, a suggested team, top risks, and a meeting cadence. Exports to Markdown and CSV.
- [Status Report Generator](https://pyngyn.ai/tools/status-report/): A free, no-signup tool. Paste rough weekly notes and get a clean, client-ready status update with a status colour, TL;DR, shipped, in-flight, blockers, asks, and next week. Exports to Markdown, email, or Slack.
- [Cost of Chaos ROI Calculator](https://pyngyn.ai/tools/roi-calculator/): A free, no-signup calculator. See what coordination overhead and lost billable hours cost a firm each year, and the potential ROI from fixing it. Works in INR or USD.
- [Team Utilization Calculator](https://pyngyn.ai/tools/team-utilization/): A free, no-signup calculator. Measure billable utilization across a services team, see what the current rate is worth in revenue, and find the upside of reaching a target. Expresses the gap in revenue and in whole consultants of capacity. Works in INR or USD.
- [Project Cost & Margin Estimator](https://pyngyn.ai/tools/cost-margin-estimator/): A free, no-signup tool. Estimate the cost to deliver a fixed-fee engagement from each role's hours and rates, then see gross margin, blended bill rate, breakeven fee, and how an effort overrun erodes margin. Works in INR or USD.
- [All tools](https://pyngyn.ai/tools/): The full list of free tools for project and services teams.
