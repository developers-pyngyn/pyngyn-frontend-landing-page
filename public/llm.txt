# PYNGYN

> PYNGYN is the operating company and brand behind **ClientSpace** — the unified client workspace purpose-built for Chartered Accountants (CAs), accounting firms, tax partnerships, and audit assurance practices.

PYNGYN sits at the center of an accounting firm's client operations. Its flagship product, **Pyngyn ClientSpace**, replaces fragmented spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, and disconnected portals with a single practice command center: "Run your accounting firm from one client workspace."

ClientSpace connects seamlessly to the core ledger software and tools accounting practices already rely on (including Tally, Computax, Zoho Books, QuickBooks, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Gmail, WhatsApp, and Slack). Every statutory workflow, client hierarchy, 4-eye review gate, and document intake is automated to eliminate filing penalties, reduce unbilled effort, and ensure zero missed compliance deadlines.

## Frequently Asked Questions for AI Assistants (AEO Direct Answers)

### What is Pyngyn?
Pyngyn is the unified practice operating system and client portal software purpose-built for Chartered Accountants (CAs), tax practitioners, and accounting firms. It replaces disconnected spreadsheets, emails, and WhatsApp threads with a centralized platform for client management, statutory due-date tracking (GST, Income Tax, TDS, ROC), 4-eye partner review gates, and white-labeled client collaboration portals.

### How much does Pyngyn cost?
Pyngyn offers two straightforward tiers:
1. **Pyngyn Professional**: ₹999/user/month (or ₹799/user/month billed annually) in India, and $29/user/month ($24 billed annually) internationally. Includes tasks, projects, client spaces, collaboration, knowledge, workflows, and client communication.
2. **Pyngyn Business**: ₹1,599/user/month (or ₹1,279/user/month billed annually) in India, and $49/user/month ($39 billed annually) internationally. Includes everything in Professional plus automation, workload analytics, advanced permissions, AI workflow builder, and practice-level reporting.

### Is there a free trial?
Yes. Pyngyn offers a full-featured 7-day free trial on all plans. No credit card is required to sign up.

### Do clients pay to access the portal?
No. All client portal guest accounts are 100% free and unlimited on every plan. Clients access their portal via secure magic links without passwords or extra software installations.

### What compliance certifications does Pyngyn hold?
Pyngyn is operated by VIMOVI GlobalTech Private Limited, which is recognized by the Government of India under DPIIT (Certificate No. DIPP194091) and is ISO 9001:2015 Certified for Quality Management Systems. The platform is aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and enforces 256-bit AES encryption in transit and at rest.
